See what a tutor sees.

I built a full SAT tutoring platform, and if there's one feature that actually defines it, it's this one — the Student Brief, the panel a tutor pulls up before every session. Answer a few questions like a student would, then switch over and see the same data from the tutor's side.

Normally a tutor burns the first chunk of a session just figuring out where a student actually stands. The Student Brief skips that — they open it already knowing, before the student even joins.

Built by Travis Feng. Happy to walk through the rest of the product directly — this page is just the one piece I could safely show on its own.
